# Poser3D

**A free, easy-to-use 3D pose tool for AI image, video and gaming work.**

Pose two 3D mannequins exactly the way you need, then export the frame as a pose reference for image-to-image or Omni image-to-video workflows, ad layouts, storyboards, fight scenes and video keyframes. Works with real people, illustrated characters and game characters: the mannequin locks the pose, the model handles the look.

👉 **Open the app:** `index.html` (or the GitHub Pages link for this repository)

## Features
- Two posable mannequins, starting seated, standing or lying side by side
- Tap any body part, then bend, twist or tilt it with sliders
- Faces and gaze lines, so head direction and eye line are always clear
- Collision handling: parts can't sink into each other, into the body or into the floor (the body adjusts when you release a slider)
- Adjustable height and build for each figure
- Save, load and undo poses (stored in your browser)
- Export PNG (transparent background) or JPG (white floor) from any camera angle

## How to use
- **Online:** open the GitHub Pages link.
- **Offline:** download `index.html` and open it in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. No install, no account.
- Drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom. Tap **Edit** or a body part to pose it. Undo with ↶ or Ctrl/Cmd+Z.
